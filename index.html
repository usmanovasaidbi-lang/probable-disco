How to play

  1. Your first book is free.
  2. Read the whole story and press “I finished reading”.
  3. Answer all 20 different questions in the book challenge.
  4. After submitting the completed test, receive 13 coins.
  5. Use your coins to unlock more books. Each new book costs 13 coins.
  6. Each book has a new set of shuffled questions when you take its test again. Coins are awarded only once per book.

Your coins, unlocked books and progress are saved in this browser on this device.
